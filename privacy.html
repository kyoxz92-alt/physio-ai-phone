Physio AI privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Physio AI is a Chrome extension that helps one physiotherapist write clinical documentation in the browser with an AI model of their choice. It is a personal tool. It has no advertising and no analytics. The only account is the optional sync sign-in described below.

What stays in your browser

Settings, API keys, memory, skills, workflows, trusted sites, past chats and the plan history are kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your own computer. Unless you turn on sync (below), they are not uploaded anywhere by the extension. API keys are never synced. A backup file is made only when you press Export, and it includes API keys or chats only if you tick those boxes.

What is sent, and to whom

  • The AI provider you choose (DeepSeek, Anthropic or ILMU) receives your instructions and the parts of the open web page the agent reads to do the task, using your own API key. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.
  • Web pages and search: when you ask the agent to look something up, it fetches public pages or DuckDuckGo results without your cookies.
  • Phone link (only when you turn it on): messages between the panel and your phone pass through a Supabase Realtime relay. Every message is encrypted on the device with a key that is shared only through the QR code, so the relay sees encrypted data only. Nothing is stored on the relay.
  • Sync between PCs (only when you sign in and turn it on): model choices, preferences, memory, skills, workflows, trusted sites and start cards, and past chats only if you tick them separately (they are off by default), are stored in a Supabase database in Singapore so your other computers can use them. Every item is encrypted on your computer (AES-256-GCM) with a key that is itself locked by your sync passphrase; the passphrase is never stored or sent. The service sees your sign-in email, item kinds, item IDs and times, but not the content. Synced chats are deleted from the service after your chosen keep-days, and in any case after 31 days. "Delete my cloud data" in Settings removes everything stored there.
  • Built-in skill updates: once a day the extension downloads the latest signed skills file (text instructions, no code) from the developer's GitHub Pages site and checks its digital signature before using it. Nothing about you is sent except the ordinary web request. You can turn this off in Settings.

Locked API keys

A copy of the extension may carry the owner's API keys in a file locked with a passphrase (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2-SHA256). The passphrase is never stored or sent. Without it the file cannot be read.

What is never done

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties beyond those listed above.
  • No patient text is ever stored unencrypted outside your computer.
  • No data is used for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to the tool's purpose.
  • No tracking, analytics or crash reporting.

Deleting your data

Delete chats or history in Settings, or remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything it stored on that computer. If you used sync, press "Delete my cloud data" in Settings → Privacy & data first to remove the encrypted copies from the sync service.

Questions: contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
